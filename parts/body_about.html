ABOUT THE PROJECT

Multilingual Food Label Parsing, Banned Dye Detection & AI Chat Assistant

The rapid growth of packaged food consumption has heightened consumer demand for transparency regarding dietary ingredients, additive safety, and nutritional value. However, modern food labels remain complex and difficult for everyday consumers to decode quickly at the point of sale. NutriScan AI is a dynamic, AI-powered web application that evaluates packaged food items using standardised health indicators — Nutri-Score (A to E) and NOVA Processing Groups (1 to 4) — bridging the gap between raw nutritional data and consumer understanding.

The platform manages a dynamic catalog of 355 packaged food products with real-time indexing, multi-category filtering and sorting. An interactive AI Specialist evaluates ingredients, highlights dietary allergens, and answers real-time queries against FSSAI guidelines and macro-nutritional targets, with full support for English, Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Malayalam and Kannada.

Classification & Evaluation Methodology

Nutrient Classification (per 100g)

  • 🟢 Sugar low ≤5g · 🟡 moderate ≤22.5g · 🔴 high >22.5g
  • 🟢 Sodium low <120mg · 🟡 moderate ≤600mg · 🔴 high >600mg
  • 🟢 Fat low ≤3g · 🟡 moderate ≤17.5g · 🔴 high >17.5g
  • 🟢 Saturated fat low ≤1.5g · 🟡 moderate ≤5g · 🔴 high >5g

Regulatory & Toxic Additive Check

Flags banned/restricted dyes and chemicals under FSSAI & WHO guidance (e.g. Rhodamine B, Metanil Yellow, Potassium Bromate INS 924, Brominated Vegetable Oil) and additives with health advisories (INS 211, INS 621/MSG, E133, E171).

Veg / Non-Veg & Allergen Detection

Automatically detects non-vegetarian markers (e.g. Carmine/E120, animal gelatin, rennet) and flags common allergens — gluten, lactose, nuts, soy, egg, sesame.

NOVA & Nutri-Score

Displays processing level (NOVA Group 1–4) and overall front-of-pack health score (Nutri-Score A–E) for every database record, with a rule-based estimate shown for freehand/OCR text that has no database match.

System Evaluation Metrics

  • OCR Text Accuracy — Character/Word Error Rate on cropped packaging labels
  • Extraction Coverage — percentage of readable nutrition tables correctly parsed
  • Class-Specific Recall (High Risk) — ensures near-zero dangerous false negatives
  • Macro F1-Score — balance across Healthy / Moderate / Unhealthy classes
  • Confusion Matrix — verifies high-risk products are never misclassified as safe
